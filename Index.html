light house

A lighthouse is a tower-like structure equipped with a powerful light
and lens system, strategically located on coastlines, hazardous reefs,
or harbor entrances to act as an essential navigational aid for mariners.

These structures, which often range from 30 to 200 feet tall, emit identifying light patterns to warn ships of danger and guide them safely to shore. While modern GPS has reduced their reliance, they remain crucial for visual navigation and safty

fort st. george

established in 1644 in Chennai (formerly Madras), was the first English fortress in India and served as the nucleus for the city's development.
Built by the British East India Company to protect its growing trade interests on the Coromandel Coast,
the fort later became the administrative headquarters for the Madras Presidency and currently houses the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly and the Secretariat.
